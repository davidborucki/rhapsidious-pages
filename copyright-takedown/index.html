1. Report a rights concern

If you believe material infringes your rights, contact us through Support with “Copyright request” in the subject, identify the work and your relationship to its owner, provide the specific clip URL or identifier, describe your concern, and include a way to contact you. Include a good-faith statement that the use is unauthorized and a statement that your information is accurate, plus your signature if submitting a formal notice. We may request additional information and share relevant parts of a complaint with the uploader to investigate. This contact is a general rights-request channel; it is not a representation that a statutory designated-agent registration has been completed.
