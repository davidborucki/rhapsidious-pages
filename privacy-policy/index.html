Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Voxxly (also identified as AudacityFM or Rhapsidious in older versions) handles information in its mobile app, website, and related services. Contact the Voxxly team through Support for privacy questions or requests.

2. Information you provide

When you register, we collect your email address, username, password, date of birth, and selected gender. We store a password hash, email-verification status, and session credentials. We also record the versions of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy acknowledged at registration and the time of that acknowledgement. If you add a profile photo or upload content, we collect the selected files and associated titles, creator or guest details, and other metadata. Reports, copyright requests, and support messages include the information you choose to send and, when available, your account or contact email.

3. Activity and technical information

We collect clip views, watch time, completion, skips, likes, saves, reposts, follows, and clips already shown to you. We store recently selected profiles to provide recent searches, plus blocks and reports to operate safety features. We use account, device, session, and playback identifiers. Our servers and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request times, browser or device information, and error or diagnostic information when delivering and protecting the service.

4. Why we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, send verification emails, display profiles and media, remember saved content, provide social features, rank and personalize the Soundbites feed, troubleshoot playback, respond to support and rights requests, and prevent abuse. Your date of birth is used for age eligibility and content restrictions. Gender is currently collected as an account field. Recommendation profiles and scores are derived from your activity and clip characteristics; these can change as you use Voxxly.

5. Public content and sharing

Your username, profile photo, uploaded posts, reposts, and follower/following lists can be visible to others, including through public profile and media links. Other people can copy or share public material outside Voxxly. Your email, date of birth, gender, and password are not displayed on your public profile. Do not include information in public media that you do not want others to see. Removing a post or blocking an account cannot retrieve copies others have already made.

6. Media processing and AI

Uploaded clips may be stored, converted into playback formats, and used to create thumbnails, transcripts, and topic or tone labels. When an administrator uses the AI metadata feature, clip media and transcripts are sent to OpenAI for transcription and content labeling. That workflow sends clip content, not your viewing history, likes, follows, or recommendation profile. Personal information spoken or shown in a clip can therefore be included in processing. Only upload media you have permission to share and process.

7. Service providers and other disclosures

We use providers for hosting, storage and media delivery (including Fly.io and Cloudflare R2), verification email (Google/Gmail), and the AI processing described above (OpenAI). They receive the information needed for their functions. We may disclose information when required by law, to address fraud or threats to safety, protect rights, or handle a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations. External podcast links and other third-party sites have their own privacy practices. The current Voxxly app does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-site advertising trackers.

8. Storage on your device

The website uses browser local storage for authentication tokens, a device identifier, and recent-profile data. The iOS app stores session credentials in the Keychain and uses local settings and media/image caches. These support sign-in and app functionality. You can sign out, clear browser storage, or remove the app to clear relevant local data; these actions do not delete your server account. Device permissions and file pickers control which photos or videos you choose to upload.

9. Retention and account deletion

We retain account and activity information while needed to provide the service, respond to requests, protect users, and meet legal obligations. Use Settings → Delete Account on mobile or the web to permanently close your account. The current deletion process revokes sessions, replaces account identifiers, clears date of birth, gender and profile-photo association, and removes linked viewing/recommendation activity, likes, saves, reposts, follows, recent searches, and blocks. It also clears the stored registration acknowledgement. Reports and support records may remain with their author link and contact email removed; text you supplied can still contain personal information. Uploaded media and stored media files are not automatically erased by account deletion. Request removal of those files or personal information in retained messages through Support; include relevant links or identifiers. Backups, delivery caches, copies made by others, and records needed for legal or safety purposes may remain after a request. Removal from backups and delivery caches may take additional time.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit your username or profile photo, remove saves or reposts, unfollow or unblock accounts, and clear recent searches through available app controls. To request access, correction, a copy, deletion of other personal information, or to object to or restrict processing where applicable, contact us through Support. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that the request concerns your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent or complaining to your privacy regulator. We will handle requests under the law that applies to you. A separate switch to disable personalized recommendations or choose a mature-content preference is not currently available.

11. Age requirements

Voxxly is for people aged 13 and over, or the higher minimum age required where they live. Minors must have permission from a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to register. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate. Date-of-birth restrictions on mature clips do not guarantee that every clip is suitable for every teenager.

12. Security and international processing

We use measures such as password hashing, access tokens, and encrypted network connections to protect information. No service can guarantee complete security. Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ. Contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your information and any rights you have concerning international processing.

13. Changes and contact

We will update the date shown here when this policy changes and provide additional notice when required for material changes. Review the current policy before providing new information. Privacy and legal requests can be sent through Support, including if you cannot sign in.
